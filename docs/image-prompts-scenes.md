# Image prompts — photographic scenes, 16 pages, 14 each

224 images. Real scenes with people and robots, not abstract geometry. This
replaces the abstract brief in image-prompts-redo.md, which produced correct
images of the wrong kind.

## STYLE BLOCK

Paste once, alone, at the start of a new chat.

```
Photoreal cinematic photograph, 16:9 landscape. Real scenes with people and robots - AI engineers working at multi-monitor desks, humanoid robots, robotic arms, modern dark offices, data centres, control rooms, factory floors. Dark near-black navy environment lit by glowing cyan and violet accent light. Shallow depth of field, volumetric haze, premium enterprise feel, photographic realism. CRITICAL FRAMING, NO CROPPING: the photograph must fill the entire 16:9 frame edge to edge with no border, no white margin, no padding and no letterboxing. Every subject must sit FULLY INSIDE the frame with clear space around it. No person, face, head, robot or object may touch, overlap or run off any edge of the frame. Never cut a head or a body at the frame edge. Compose wide so nothing is cut off. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT of any kind: no words, letters, numbers, labels, captions, headings, titles, watermarks, logos, brand marks, and no readable text on any screen or monitor - screens may glow with abstract light only. This is NOT a poster, NOT a banner, NOT a slide, NOT an infographic and NOT a screenshot.
```

Send one subject line at a time — the words after the dash only, never the
filename. Every line already says "wide shot" or similar, because the fault
being designed out is content cut off at the frame edge.
